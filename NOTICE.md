# AEGIS notices

AEGIS is a hackathon prototype for emergency planning and decision support. It
is not a certified forecasting, hydraulic, structural, casualty, navigation, or
evacuation-ordering system.

The interface separates imported context, estimated inputs, simulated effects,
and optional model-authored text. Public map and incident providers remain the
owners of their respective data. Attribution is preserved in the interface and
the data-provenance documentation.

The source is publicly reviewable under the repository's all-rights-reserved
license. Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses.
